RUST CIRCUIT PRIVACY POLICY

Version 1. This policy explains what happens to information when you play Rust Circuit, made by Inkdrop Interactive ("we"). We have no accounts or servers of our own, and we don't receive your game data. The game has no ads.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

The name you type, your settings, saved races and a random ID the game makes to recognise your device when it rejoins a game. Uninstalling the game removes them.

PLAYING ON THE SAME WI-FI (LAN)

Nothing goes over the internet. Your name, your random device ID and your moves go to the other devices in your game, and they can see your device's local network address. If you host, your device also announces your name and your game on the local network while the game is open.

PLAYING ONLINE

Nothing is sent to Epic until you open ONLINE GAME. From then on the game uses Epic Online Services, from Epic Games, to sign your device in and connect you with other players. There is no Epic account to create or log in to.

Epic handles:
- an anonymous device credential, and the player ID Epic issues for it;
- your IP address, as part of any internet connection;
- your game's invite code, and which player ID is hosting it;
- service measurements: a random session ID, the number of requests, response times and status codes;
- the game's traffic, when a connection between players has to be relayed through Epic's servers.

Epic and its service providers may process this in the United States and other countries. Epic describes its own handling in its privacy policy: https://www.epicgames.com/site/privacypolicy

Other players in your online game receive your name, your random device ID and your moves. When your devices connect directly, they can also see your IP address.

THE UNITY ENGINE

The game is made with Unity, whose engine may send Unity basic information about the device and the game, such as hardware type and operating system. Unity describes this in its privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

WHY

We use this information only to run the game and its multiplayer, which is the basis on which it is processed. We don't sell it, and we don't use it for advertising.

HOW LONG

Things on your device last until you uninstall the game. A saved hosted race is deleted when the race is won or the host closes it. Epic keeps the data it handles according to its own policy.

YOUR CHOICES AND RIGHTS

You can play alone or on the same Wi-Fi without using Epic's services. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to its use. We hold none of it ourselves, but write to us and we will help you with requests about the player ID Epic issued for your device. You can also contact Epic, or complain to your local data protection authority; in Poland, that is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

CHILDREN

The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the game will show you the new version.
