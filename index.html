About me

I’ve been a gamer for most of my life. It’s what first got me interested in computers, and it led to a career in IT that’s now lasted over 20 years. Through all of that I’ve seen games as an art form, a way of telling stories and sharing ideas, except you get to be part of them.

When choosing the name Inkdrop, I wanted something that reflected the fact that most of my ideas start out as ink on paper and also the creativity I believe goes into every game. That first drop of ink is where it all begins.

“A drop of ink may make a million think.”

Lord Byron
