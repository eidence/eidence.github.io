INKDROP INTERACTIVE PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 1 November 2026. This policy covers the mobile games made by Inkdrop Interactive ("we"): Beanville and Rust Circuit. It explains what happens to information when you play them. Neither game requires an account, and we do not independently store personal data. We don't sell your information.

BEANVILLE

Information collected. The following is collected automatically through third-party services: device type and operating system version; general geographic region (country level, not precise location); app session duration and frequency; in-game progression and events, such as levels reached and score; and app version. We do not collect your name, email address, precise location or payment information.

How it is used. Solely to understand how the game is used in order to improve gameplay and performance, to identify and fix technical issues, and to measure general engagement. We do not use it for direct marketing.

Legal basis in the European Economic Area (EEA). We process this data on the basis of our legitimate interests in improving the game and understanding user behaviour. This processing does not override your fundamental rights and freedoms. For advertising features, processing is based on your consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

Third-party services. Beanville uses these services, which collect and process data under their own privacy policies:
- GameAnalytics: https://gameanalytics.com/privacy
- Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy

These services may transfer and process data in countries outside the EEA. Where required, appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses are in place.

Retention. Data is retained in accordance with the policies of those services.

Opting out. You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the game.

RUST CIRCUIT

Rust Circuit has no ads. We have no servers for it.

What stays on your device. The name you type, your settings, saved races and a random ID the game makes to recognise your device when it rejoins a game. Uninstalling the game removes them.

Playing on the same Wi-Fi (LAN). Nothing goes over the internet. Your name, your random device ID and your moves go to the other devices in your game, and they can see your device's local network address. If you host, your device also announces your name and your game on the local network while the game is open.

Playing online. Nothing is sent to Epic until you open ONLINE GAME. From then on the game uses Epic Online Services, from Epic Games, to sign your device in and connect you with other players. There is no Epic account to create or log in to. Epic handles:
- an anonymous device credential, and the player ID Epic issues for it;
- your IP address, as part of any internet connection;
- your game's invite code, and which player ID is hosting it;
- if you host a public game, its track and how many seats are taken and free, which anyone playing can see in the list of games;
- service measurements: a random session ID, the number of requests, response times and status codes;
- the game's traffic, when a connection between players has to be relayed through Epic's servers.

Epic and its service providers may process this in the United States and other countries. Epic describes its own handling in its privacy policy: https://www.epicgames.com/site/privacypolicy

Other players in your online game receive your name, your random device ID and your moves, and in a public game those players can be strangers. When your devices connect directly, they can also see your IP address.

The Unity engine. The game is made with Unity, whose engine may send Unity basic information about the device and the game, such as hardware type and operating system. Unity describes this in its privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Analytics. Rust Circuit may use GameAnalytics to show us how the game is played, collecting automatically: device type and operating system version; general geographic region (country level, not precise location); session duration and frequency; in-game progression and events; and app version. It doesn't include the name you type, your email address, your precise location or payment information. GameAnalytics handles this under its own privacy policy, and may process it outside the EEA: https://gameanalytics.com/privacy

How it is used. To run the game and its multiplayer, which you ask for by playing it. Analytics are used to improve gameplay and performance and to fix technical issues, on the basis of our legitimate interests in doing so. We don't use any of this for advertising.

Retention. Things on your device last until you uninstall the game. A saved hosted race is deleted when the race is won or the host closes it. Epic and GameAnalytics keep the data they handle according to their own policies.

Purchases. In-game content is sold through Google Play, which handles the payment under Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. The game asks Google Play what your account owns and keeps the answer on your device. We don't receive your payment details.

Your choices. You can play alone or on the same Wi-Fi without using Epic's services. You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the game.

YOUR RIGHTS

If you are in the EEA, you have the right to access the personal data held about you, request its correction or deletion, object to or restrict its processing, request its portability, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, and complain to your local data protection authority. In Poland, that is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

If you are in California, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, request its deletion, and opt out of its sale. We do not sell personal data.

To use these rights, contact us. We hold no personal data ourselves, so we will help you with requests to the services named above, such as the player ID Epic issued for your device. You can also contact those services directly.

CHILDREN

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through one of our games, contact us and we will promptly help to have it deleted.

SECURITY

We rely on industry-standard security measures provided by our third-party service providers to protect data during transmission and storage.

CHANGES

We may update this policy. Changes are posted at https://inkdropinteractive.com/privacy.html with a new date, and Rust Circuit will show you the new version.
